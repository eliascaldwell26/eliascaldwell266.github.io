  • Shooting: hold Space / Shoot for roughly half a second: gather, rise, then release in green near the top of your jump. The meter and jump move together, and the player lands quickly after release. Holding past green gives a late miss. Rookie has the widest base window; Legend has the narrowest. Nearby defenders shrink the window, especially in front with raised hands. The contest shown when you release locks in your timing. Early and late releases hit the backboard and bounce out for a live rebound. Long shots score 3; inside shots and dunks score 2. Shots from your own end have a much smaller green and a low chance even with perfect timing. A full-court heave is never guaranteed by Boost.
  • Passing: aim with a direction and press Pass. Control follows the receiving teammate. Each pass has one 5% interception chance, regardless of how many defenders cross it. Catches have brief steal protection. Alt switches to your teammate closest to the ball, including while a pass or rebound is in the air. Tab also switches while the court has focus.
  • Dribbling: automatic ball bounces plus C crossover, X 360 spin and V step back. Step back creates space away from the attacking basket. Skill moves use stamina and briefly protect possession.
  • Defense: tap or hold E or JUMP / BLOCK while the opponent gathers. The button selects your closest teammate already near the shooter; an early press briefly readies the block and times the jump with the release. Stay within one small step for the raised hands to swat the rising ball. You cannot swat distant shots, descending shots or shots near the rim. Steals have a windup and recovery; time them when the ball is low.
  • Competitions: open TOURNAMENT or FRANCHISE from the lobby. Tournament is an 8- or 16-team knockout. Franchise has 29 regular-season games, best-of-three playoffs, lineup management, trades and a fictional rookie draft. Play or simulate games; progress saves on this browser. Open BOX SCORE during or after a game for every player’s statistics.
  • Players: names and positions are under each player. Toggle PLAYER NAME TAGS in setup or the Locker Room to hide or show them. Listed height changes body size and reach. The corner shows your controlled player’s three strongest custom gameplay skills. Three-point stars widen the long-shot green; finishing stars help layups. Taller centers have longer reach and strong finishing and blocking.
  • CPU threes: close defensive pressure sharply lowers conversion even on Legend. Open shots remain dangerous; guarded shots retain a small chance. A made three triggers cheering fans, arena LEDs and a celebration. Enable SOUND for the crowd cheer.
  • The buzzer: a shot released before 0:00 remains live. The game waits for the basket or the missed ball to settle. You cannot start a new attempt after the horn. A tying basket sends the game to sudden-death overtime.
  • Dunks: release green close to the rim with a strong finisher to drive up, extend the ball, slam it through and land after a short rim hang. Tall finishers can dunk from a standstill; smaller athletic finishers need forward momentum. The ball must leave the hands before the buzzer to count.
  • Real Player %: choose this shooting mode in setup or the Locker Room to turn the meter and release timing off. Tap Space / Shoot once; your player gathers, jumps and releases automatically. The chance badge shows the adjusted odds and NBA base rate for that zone. NBA 2025–26 shooting data is used where available, with 2024–25 for absent players. Missing zones use the player's 2P/3P average; players with no NBA sample use labeled estimates. Coverage, movement, fatigue and long distance lower the odds. Boost does not guarantee a percentage-mode basket.
  • Ankle breakers & upgrades: use C crossover, X spin or V stepback near a defender to make them stumble or fall. Handles and defensive ability affect the chance; a recovery cooldown prevents repeated knockdowns. Open ANIMATION SHOP → PLAYER UPGRADES to buy five levels of Shooting Lab, Ankle Breaker or Conditioning with earned coins. Training applies to your home team; Watch Game uses neutral teams.
  • Shot meter: open the Locker Room to set its size from 80% to 250%. The preview compares open and heavily guarded green windows at your selected difficulty. Green stays at the tip; meter size changes the display size.
  • Fullscreen: F or ⛶. P pauses. The clock freezes while you customize a player or read the playbook.
  • Touch: use the four-direction pad, Jump / Block, Pass and Shoot below the court. Local two-player mode shares a keyboard.

Online: choose PLAY ONLINE, create a 1v1 or 5v5 party, share its code and both READY UP. The host starts. Each person uses the first-player controls; in 5v5 AI fills your other four spots and Alt switches control. Keep the host’s tab open. Open ONLINE to reconnect or leave.

Celebrations: preview 30 effects in the Animation Shop and set CELEBRATION SIZE from 100% to 200%. Your choice saves automatically.
